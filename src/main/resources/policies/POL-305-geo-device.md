title: Geolocation and Device Trust Standard
---

## 1. Impossible Travel

Two successful authorisations on the same card are considered physically
impossible when the straight-line distance between them exceeds the distance
coverable by commercial air travel in the elapsed time (approximately 900 km
per hour). Confirmed impossible travel is treated as near-conclusive evidence
of card-present counterfeit or cloned-card use.

## 2. Cross-Border Transactions

Cross-border card-present transactions are elevated risk when the destination
country differs from the cardholder's country of residence and there is no
travel notification on file. Cross-border card-not-present transactions
originating from high-risk corridors warrant additional scrutiny but are not
alone sufficient to deny a dispute.

## 3. Device Trust

Transactions from devices never before associated with the account, devices
younger than 72 hours, or devices exhibiting emulator or proxy indicators are
untrusted. Three or more prior fraud reports against a device fingerprint make
the device presumptively hostile. A transaction from an untrusted or hostile
device strongly corroborates the cardholder's claim of unauthorized use.
