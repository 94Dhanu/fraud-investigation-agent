title: First-Party Fraud and Serial Disputers
---

## 1. First-Party Fraud Indicators

A dispute pattern suggests first-party (friendly) fraud when the cardholder
has filed 3 or more prior disputes, the disputed merchant is in a low-risk
category, the transaction device is a recognised device in good standing, and
no corroborating technical evidence of compromise exists.

## 2. Serial Disputer Threshold

Cardholders with 3 or more prior disputes within 12 months are classified as
serial disputers. Their claims require corroborating technical evidence before
approval; absent such evidence the recommended action is to request
additional information rather than an outright denial.

## 3. Denial Criteria

A dispute may only be denied when the weight of evidence indicates the
transaction was authorised or benefited the cardholder: recognised trusted
device, consistent geography, normal velocity, low-risk merchant, and a
history consistent with first-party fraud. Denials must cite the specific
evidence relied upon.
