## 1. Merchant Risk Bands

Merchant categories classified as high risk include: gambling, cryptocurrency
exchanges, money transfer services, adult services, prepaid phone cards, and
digital goods resellers. Merchants in high-risk categories carry a baseline
risk premium in every fraud assessment.

## 2. Chargeback Rate Thresholds

A merchant's rolling 90-day chargeback rate above 1.5% is elevated; above 4%
is excessive and triggers acquirer review. Disputes against merchants with
excessive chargeback rates should weigh merchant-side compromise as a likely
explanation.

## 3. Internal Watchlist

Merchants on the internal watchlist (acquirer risk tier D) are subject to
enhanced due diligence. Any dispute involving a watchlisted merchant must be
escalated to a senior investigator and cannot be auto-denied.
