title: Human Review and Escalation Workflow
---

## 1. Mandatory Human Review

Every automated fraud assessment is advisory only. No dispute may be approved,
denied, or escalated without a decision recorded by a human reviewer. The
reviewer may approve the agent's recommendation, override it with a written
justification, or request additional information from the customer.

## 2. Escalation Criteria

Cases must be escalated to a senior investigator when ANY of the following
hold: (a) overall risk score at or above 70, (b) impossible travel confirmed,
(c) card-testing pattern confirmed, (d) watchlisted merchant involved, or
(e) account-takeover indicators present (both credential and contact changes
within 30 days).

## 3. Audit and Record Keeping

Every action on a case — intake, evidence collection, assessment, review
decision, and resolution — must be recorded in the append-only audit trail
with actor and timestamp. Audit records must never be modified or deleted.
Reviewer justifications are mandatory for overrides and are part of the
permanent case record.
